On the specific risks you raised:
- **Splits:** the minute data is unadjusted, but levels more than 3% from the prior close are filtered out, so splits only cost a few touches.
- **Clustering:** resampling by day instead of by symbol-day changes the intervals by less than 0.2 bps.
- **Placebo timing:** controlling for time of touch doesn't change the break results.
- **Bid/ask:** buying at the ask and selling at the bid is handled correctly.
