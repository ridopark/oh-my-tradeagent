I rebuilt C1, C2 and the key C4 numbers independently, and they match yours exactly. The main conclusion, no tradeable edge on the author's levels, survives. Two of the supporting claims don't.

**Verdicts**
- **C1 HOLDS.** r60 is −6.3 (FIT) / −4.6 (TEST). The placebo flaw is real: 50% of fired placebo legs gap vs 4% of real ones, because offsets are anchored to the prior close. But a gap-matched placebo (offset from today's open) puts real legs *below* placebo: r60 −17 FIT, and r5 TEST −11 to −14 with the CI excluding 0. If anything there's a slight short-horizon fade, not continuation.
- **C2 HOLDS.** 0/64, and best-on-FIT is −10.2 on TEST. Still 0/64 when target is checked before stop on the same bar.
- **C3 HOLDS, weakly evidenced.** I get 43.7% vs 31% in my own placebos, and the best single rule (PMH) hits 16.5%. There's no code behind your 33% placebo or "best rule 33%". 94% of levels are integers or .5, a rule you never tested; my best round-number rule hits 24%.
- **C4: "no survivor" HOLDS; the gap-through candidate is noise.** p=0.061 comes from an unstudentized White RC (`evalh.py:15`), not the Hansen-style test PREREG2 specified. Studentized, it is p=0.59. Its FIT half is weak (median −25 bps, 4/9 positive), it includes a typo leg, and generic premarket-level gap-throughs 2016–2025 show no support (NVDA −47 bps, n=32). "Min RC p 0.58 intraday" only reproduces on an intraday-only family; over the full ledger it's 0.92.
- **C5:** the MDE is right only for the round-1 intraday arm (23–39 bps); for gap-through it's ~260+ bps. **The cost claim is unsupported.** No quote data or code produces the 84 bps. Inferred from option trade prices, spreads are about 378 bps of premium, roughly 10 bps of underlying. Overnight theta (~15–30 bps) is ignored.
- **C6 WEAKENED.** The numbers reproduce only by excluding RT01. Unclosed trades are handled one-sidedly: the AMD alert (RT08), modelled as −99.6%, actually traded at 7.50–11.45, consistent with the author's "2.50 to 8.00" claim. Depending on how open trades are marked, the mean ranges from −2% to +19%.
